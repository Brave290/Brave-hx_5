Our Story

Crafting Visual Stories That Inspire

At Brave-hx, we believe every brand has a unique story waiting to be told through design. Founded with a passion for visual excellence, our team of dedicated creatives combines artistic vision with strategic thinking.

From startups to established businesses, we've helped brands across various industries create memorable visual identities that resonate with their target audience and stand out in today's competitive market.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a typical project take?

Logo designs typically take 3-5 business days. Full brand identity projects take 7-14 business days. Timelines vary based on project complexity and scope.

Do you offer revisions?

Yes! All our packages include revisions. Our Professional and Enterprise packages include unlimited revisions to ensure you're completely satisfied.

What file formats do you deliver?

We deliver files in all major formats including AI, PSD, PDF, PNG, JPG, SVG, and EPS. We also provide web-optimized and print-ready versions.

How do I get started?

Simply fill out the contact form above or reach out via WhatsApp. We'll schedule a consultation to discuss your project and provide a custom quote.

Do you work with international clients?

Absolutely! We work with clients worldwide. Communication is handled via email, WhatsApp, or video calls for your convenience.
